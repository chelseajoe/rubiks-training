      Steps

      F2L is ranked by regrips first, then turning speed. A grip model follows both hands through every alg: R and L wrists, U with whichever index finger is free, sledgehammer-style F, ring fingers for D and B. A regrip costs about four quick turns, so a slightly longer alg with no regrips wins. Both hands are used equally, whichever is faster.
